About

Design-first, detail-obsessed.

I'm a graphic designer from Mumbai, specializing in brand identities, packaging design, ad creatives, and social media visuals. I work primarily with CorelDRAW, Adobe Illustrator, and Photoshop to craft visuals that stand out.

Alongside design, I also build responsive websites with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — so brands I work with can get a complete visual and digital presence.

I'm continuously refining my craft and learning new design trends to deliver high-quality work on every project.

Goal

To become a professional freelance graphic designer and help brands build a strong visual identity — from logo to packaging to ads — with a supporting web presence.
